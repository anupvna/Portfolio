Carnatic music

Carnatic music is where I first fell for patterns. Every swara is a clean frequency ratio, every raga follows rules, and every tala is a cycle that wraps around. Writing code scratches the same itch. Tap a swara or use your keyboard to hear it, and see its ratio to Sa.

Raga: Mayamalavagowla, the first raga most students learn

Keys S R G M P D N, then K for high Sa

Did you know? All 72 melakarta ragas come from simple counting: 6 ways to choose Ri and Ga, times 6 ways to choose Da and Ni, times 2 choices of Ma. 6 × 6 × 2 = 72. It's an enumeration problem that's centuries old.
